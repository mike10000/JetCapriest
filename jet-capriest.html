JET CAPRIEST

Musician • Producer • Sing-writer

About

Jet Capriest is a singer, multi-instrumentalist, and bandleader from the Washington, D.C. area whose sound blends soul, gospel, R&B, and contemporary pop with a distinctive emotional edge.

Known for his rich tone, Jet has built a reputation as a performer who can both energize a room and create intimate, memorable musical moments.
