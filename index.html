The Band

First Sunday is a dynamic live band from Prince George's County, Maryland, known for delivering high-energy performances that blend musical excellence with crowd-pleasing versatility.

Formed in 2016 (originally as Thee Unknowns), the group has evolved into an ensemble capable of moving seamlessly across genres — Pop, classic rock, country, R&B, Jazz, and contemporary hits.

Perfect for:

  • Corporate Events
  • Weddings & Receptions
  • Private Parties
  • Festivals & Concerts
  • Club & Bar Venues
